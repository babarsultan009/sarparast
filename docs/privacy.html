Sarparast — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026. Sarparast is made by Nexvron ("we"). This policy covers the Sarparast app for Android and the Sarparast website (sarparast.nexyvron.workers.dev).

Who holds your data

Each school runs Sarparast on its own computer. Student, parent and staff records — names, classes, attendance, results, fees, messages and documents — are stored on that school's computer and are controlled by the school. The app shows you the records the school has given your login access to. Questions about these records should go to your school.

What the app and website collect

  • Sign-in: your school ID, email or phone and password, sent to your school's server to sign you in. Your password is never stored on your phone.
  • Connection details: when you use Sarparast, your school's server notes the time, whether you used the app or a browser, and your phone's model and Android version, so the school and Sarparast support can help when something does not work.
  • Help requests: what you write in Help & support, with the result of "Test my connection", is sent to Sarparast support (Nexvron) so we can answer you.
  • Family Pass payments: for a payment in the app, Google Play handles your payment details; we receive only whether the pass is active. For a payment on the website, JazzCash or Easypaisa handle your payment details; we receive the transaction reference and amount. For a transfer, you send us the Transaction ID and, if you choose, your phone number.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data or show advertising.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone. Photos or files you choose to upload (for example a fee receipt) go only to your school.

Sharing

Data is shared only with your school, with Nexvron for support and the Family Pass, and with the payment provider you choose (Google Play, JazzCash or Easypaisa), as described above.

Security

All connections use HTTPS encryption. Each school's data stays on that school's own computer and is backed up by the school.

Children

Student logins are created by the school. A parent or the school can ask for a student's login to be removed at any time.

Deleting your data

Ask your school to delete your login and records, or write to Sarparast support from Help & support in the app; we will pass the request to your school and delete any help requests we hold.

Contact

Nexvron — Sarparast support, through Help & support in the app or website.
